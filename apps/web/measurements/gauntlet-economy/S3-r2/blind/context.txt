Three pairs from two study apps. In each pair the two frames are the same moment, at the same
viewport and in the same colour scheme, so the comparison is of how the screen looks and feels
rather than of what it is doing.

Pair 1, A.png and B.png. The course laid out as a run of lessons: the screen a student opens to
see where they are and to work out which single lesson to do next. Phone shape 390x844 at 3x,
light scheme, nothing pressed, the screen settled.

Pair 2, A-2.png and B-2.png. One question inside a lesson, on screen, with nothing answered yet
and no feedback shown. Phone shape 390x844 at 3x, dark scheme, nothing pressed.

Pair 3, A-3.png and B-3.png. The end of a lesson: the screen that reports what the student just
earned. Phone shape 390x844 at 3x, dark scheme, 2500 ms after the screen opened, so both frames
are settled rather than mid animation.

The two schemes are not a choice: the committed captures hold the course screen in light and the
lesson and reward screens in dark, so each pair matches the scheme it was available in. Compare
within a pair, not across pairs.

Both sides are unmodified captures. Nothing was painted out of either frame: no text was removed,
no wordmark or logo was found on any of the six frames, and no character, mascot or illustration
was touched on either side.

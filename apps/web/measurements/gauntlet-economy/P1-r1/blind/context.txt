This is the feedback moment inside a lesson on a phone: after the student submits an answer, the app tells them whether it was right or wrong and the mascot reacts. Frame 1 is the response to a correct answer, frame 2 the response to a wrong answer, and frames 3 to 6 are a burst at 0, 400, 900 and 2500 ms after a streak of correct answers in a row triggers a celebration from the mascot with a speech bubble.
